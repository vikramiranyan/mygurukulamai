Gurukulam AI Privacy

Gurukulam AI is designed as a parent-controlled learning environment. Parent account identity is used to authenticate the account. Child profiles and learning configuration are controlled by the authenticated parent.

Google services

If a parent grants Google Drive access, Gurukulam AI uses the least-privilege Drive scope needed to create and manage the app's own files. Drive data is not sold or used for advertising.

Learning data

Parent-entered child profiles, timetable information, textbook chapter data, teaching plans, homework, tests and learning progress are used to provide the requested learning experience. The application is designed to keep this data scoped to the parent's account and child.

Security

Authentication is verified, access tokens are short-lived, browser security policies are enabled, and authenticated Drive requests are made over HTTPS.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the support contact configured for the Gurukulam AI Google Cloud project.

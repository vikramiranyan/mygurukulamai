Gurukulam AI Terms

Gurukulam AI is an educational assistance product intended to support parent-guided learning. It does not replace a school, teacher, parent or qualified professional.

Parent responsibility

Parents are responsible for the child profile, uploaded learning material, permissions granted to connected services, and reviewing learning content where appropriate.

AI limitations

AI-generated explanations can be incomplete or incorrect. Important academic, safety or wellbeing decisions should be independently checked by a responsible adult.

Connected services

When Google Drive is enabled, access is governed by Google's terms and the permissions shown during authorization. Gurukulam AI requests only the permissions necessary for its Drive features.

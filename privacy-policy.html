Privacy Policy

Effective date: 30 August 2026

Gurukulam AI (“we”, “us”, or “the app”) is an educational application designed to provide a parent-controlled learning environment for children.

Information we use

When you sign in with Google, we use the Google account information required to authenticate your account, such as your email address and basic profile information. When you authorize Google Drive, the app uses the permissions you grant to store and retrieve Gurukulam AI data in Google Drive.

Google Drive data

Gurukulam AI is designed to use the limited Google Drive drive.file permission. This permission is intended to let the app work with files created or explicitly made available to it, rather than providing unrestricted access to your entire Drive.

Drive data is used only to provide Gurukulam AI features such as saving and loading your application data. We do not sell your Google data or use it for advertising.

Data storage and control

Your Gurukulam AI application data is intended to remain under your control in your Google Drive. You can revoke the app’s Google access from your Google Account security settings. If access is revoked, Gurukulam AI cannot continue to read or write the protected Drive data until authorization is restored.

Data sharing

We do not sell personal information. We do not share Google user data with third parties except where necessary to operate the service, comply with law, or protect the security and integrity of the service.

Children and parents

Gurukulam AI provides parent-controlled features for managing children’s learning information. Parents are responsible for the information they enter into the application and for ensuring that use of the service is appropriate for their family.

Security

We use reasonable technical measures appropriate to the application to protect information. No internet service can guarantee absolute security.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the service or its data practices change. The latest version will be published on this page with an updated effective date.

Contact

For privacy questions regarding Gurukulam AI, contact the developer using the support contact shown on the Google authorization screen.
